# The method's sources are the authority for planner rules

Every planner rule follows norwegiansingles.run and sirpoc's posts (see `docs/research/nsm-rationale.md`), not any other NSM calculator. Where the sources leave a number or procedure open (the 23% base target, the 35-minute session cap, merging short Easy Runs, the Rep Format list and others listed in the rationale), the planner picks one, records the reason in the rationale and labels it *planner choice* on the How this works page, so a runner can tell the method from the app's interpretation of it.
