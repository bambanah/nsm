# Session Choices are the only per-Week state

Supersedes 0001. Weeks are still derived from Plan Settings and their Monday date and never stored, but the runner can now pick the Rep Format for any of a Week's Sub-threshold Sessions. Those picks are stored as Session Choices keyed by Week and weekday and applied after derivation. Whenever Plan Settings are saved, including a Reshuffle, every Week with Session Choices is re-derived and any choice whose day is no longer a Sub-threshold Session, or whose Rep Format exceeds the per-session cap, is deleted, so a Session Choice never lingers waiting for its day to come back. We rejected storing whole Weeks (needs reconciling every run with Plan Settings changes, not just the chosen sessions), per-weekday choices in Plan Settings (the same session every Week loses the variety the sources want) and client-only choices (lost on reload and invisible to a future Sync).
